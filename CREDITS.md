# Credits

Standing Tank Defense is inspired by Nethalythic's Warcraft III custom map *Tower Survivors*. All creatures, names, abilities, art, and flavor text shipped in this product are original works created for Standing Tank Defense. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment or Hasbro, and uses no trademarks, characters, or assets owned by them.

## Audio

Sound effects in `godot/audio/sfx/` are curated and processed (mono downmix,
trim, loudness normalization — see `godot/audio/fetch_sfx.py` for the exact
per-file source mapping) from the following **Kenney** packs, all released
under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain — no attribution required; credited
here with thanks anyway):

- [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds)
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio)
- [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio)

The music stems in `godot/audio/music/` and the victory/defeat stingers are
original procedural compositions generated by `godot/audio/gen_music.py`
(original works created for Standing Tank Defense).
